Diagram answer keys

Use after attempting the numbered blank worksheets. The numbers identify label positions, not necessarily sequential steps.

01-Homeostasis

  1. Temperature deviates
  2. Receptors detect change
  3. Control center compares
  4. Too hot: sweating and skin vasodilation
  5. Too cold: shivering and skin vasoconstriction
  6. Temperature approaches range
  7. Corrective drive decreases

02-Cross-Bridge-Cycle

  1. Energized myosin binds exposed actin
  2. Power stroke
  3. ATP binds; myosin detaches
  4. ATP hydrolysis repositions the myosin head

03-Sarcomere

  1. Z disc
  2. Thin filament
  3. Thick filament
  4. M line
  5. Shorter Z-to-Z distance

04-Tonicity

  1. Hypotonic
  2. Isotonic
  3. Hypertonic
  4. Water enters; swelling
  5. No sustained net volume change
  6. Water exits; shrinking

05-Action-Potential

  1. Resting potential
  2. Threshold
  3. Na+ entry: depolarization
  4. Na+ inactivation and K+ exit: repolarization
  5. After-hyperpolarization
