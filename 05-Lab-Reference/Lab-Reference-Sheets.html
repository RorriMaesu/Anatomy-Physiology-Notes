Tonicity

External solutionNet water tendencyTypical animal-cell response
HypotonicInto cellSwelling
IsotonicNo sustained net volume changeApproximately stable
HypertonicOut of cellShrinking

These predictions depend on effectively nonpenetrating solutes. Osmolarity and tonicity are not always interchangeable. Water molecules continue moving in isotonic conditions.
