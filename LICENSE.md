These study notes adapt material from J. Gordon Betts and colleagues, *Anatomy and Physiology 2e*, OpenStax (2022).

Access the original textbook for free: https://openstax.org/details/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e

The adapted study material in this repository is distributed under Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial–ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

Changes include condensed explanations, reorganized section plans, mnemonic cues, original practice questions, original schematic diagrams, and an interactive adaptation of Figure 1.12 for the Anatomy Atlas. The atlas adaptation replaces the original labels with numbered markers and adds quiz behavior, accepted-answer logic, spelling guidance, and memory aids. These materials are study aids rather than reproductions of the complete textbook. Preserve attribution, identify your changes, and follow the license terms when sharing adaptations.

See `07-Sources/Sources-and-Attribution.md` for section-level source links and additional study-method references. Linked third-party resources retain their respective terms. This is an independent study collection, not an official OpenStax publication.
