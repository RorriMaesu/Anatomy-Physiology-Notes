Chapter 17 memory cues

Mnemonics11 sectionsPrint friendly

17.1 An Overview of the Endocrine System

Endocrine enters internal fluids. Explain why only some cells respond to a hormone.

17.2 Hormones

Receptor location follows hormone properties. Compare peptide and steroid signaling.

17.3 The Pituitary Gland and Hypothalamus

Posterior stores/releases; hypothalamus synthesizes ADH and oxytocin.

17.4 The Thyroid Gland

TRH to TSH to thyroid hormones. Draw negative-feedback arrows.

17.5 The Parathyroid Glands

PTH puts calcium higher. Predict the response to falling calcium.

17.6 The Adrenal Glands

GFR = Glomerulosa, Fasciculata, Reticularis; salt, sugar, sex. Keep medulla separate.

17.7 The Pineal Gland

Melatonin marks biological night. Explain timing rather than calling it a universal sleep switch.

17.8 Gonadal and Placental Hormones

Explain one reproductive feedback loop using source, target, and effect.

17.9 The Endocrine Pancreas

Beta brings glucose down; alpha adds it to blood. Explain the mechanisms.

17.10 Organs with Secondary Endocrine Functions

Organ plus signal plus target. Explain a hormone made outside a classical endocrine gland.

17.11 Development and Aging of the Endocrine System

Distinguish an age-associated tendency from an inevitable disease.
