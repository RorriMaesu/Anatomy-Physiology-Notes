Mnemonics6 sectionsPrint friendly

20.1 Structure and Function of Blood Vessels

Arteries away; veins toward the heart. Connect wall structure to pressure handling.

20.2 Blood Flow, Blood Pressure, and Resistance

Flow follows a pressure difference. Predict narrowing an arteriole's effect.

20.3 Capillary Exchange

Push versus pull, naming the compartment. Explain edema using the course model.

20.4 Homeostatic Regulation of the Vascular System

Draw a low-pressure feedback loop. Separate local metabolic and systemic control.

20.5 Circulatory Pathways

Portal links two capillary beds. Trace assigned routes without skipping branches.

20.6 Development of Blood Vessels and Fetal Circulation

Ductus venosus, foramen ovale, ductus arteriosus. Explain what each bypasses.
