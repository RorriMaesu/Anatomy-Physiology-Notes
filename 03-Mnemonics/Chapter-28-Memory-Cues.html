Chapter 28 memory cues

Mnemonics7 sectionsPrint friendly

28.1 Fertilization

One sperm contribution restores the diploid complement. Explain why polyspermy is prevented.

28.2 Embryonic Development

Morula mass; blastocyst cavity. Order stages and explain germ-layer formation.

28.3 Fetal Development

Embryonic organization, fetal growth/maturation as a broad distinction. Explain placental exchange without direct blood mixing.

28.4 Changes During Pregnancy, Labor, and Birth

Stretch reinforces contractions through the labor pathway. Identify the loop's endpoint.

28.5 Adjustments of the Infant at Birth and Postnatal Stages

Lungs open; circulation redirects. Explain postnatal fetal-shunt changes.

28.6 Lactation

Prolactin produces; oxytocin ejects. Draw both responses.

28.7 Patterns of Inheritance

Genotype allele combination; phenotype expression. Solve crosses and state assumptions.
