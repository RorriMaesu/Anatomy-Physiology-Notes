Mnemonics5 sectionsPrint friendly

19.1 Heart Anatomy

Right to lungs; left to body. Trace blood through chambers and valves.

19.2 Cardiac Muscle and Electrical Activity

SA to AV to bundle to branches to Purkinje. Distinguish electrical and mechanical events.

19.3 Cardiac Cycle

Pressure opens and closes valves. Predict valve state from pressure differences.

19.4 Cardiac Physiology

CO = HR x SV. Explain each variable's distinct effect.

19.5 Development of the Heart

Draw sequential stages. Explain how a partitioning defect changes flow.
