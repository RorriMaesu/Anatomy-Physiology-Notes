# Chapter 15 memory cues

Thoracolumbar sympathetic; craniosacral parasympathetic. Compare chain anatomy.

## 15.2 Autonomic Reflexes and Homeostasis

Same organ, different receptor effects. Predict heart, pupil, airway, and digestive responses.

## 15.3 Central Control

Hypothalamus coordinates homeostasis. Connect emotion to an autonomic response.

## 15.4 Drugs that Affect the Autonomic System

Agonist activates; antagonist blocks. Predict effects from receptor action.
