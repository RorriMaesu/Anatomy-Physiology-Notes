Chapter 6 memory cues

6.1 The Functions of the Skeletal System

“Support Protects Movers; Minerals, Blood, Fat” recalls support, protection, movement, mineral storage, hematopoiesis, and fat storage.

Precision check: Bone is living, vascular tissue. A skeleton is not merely a static framework of mineral.

6.2 Bone Classification

“Long Shorts Fit Irregular Shapes” = Long, Short, Flat, Irregular, Sesamoid. A tiny phalanx is still a long bone.

Precision check: A flat bone can be curved. “Long bone” does not mean every physically large bone belongs in that category.

6.3 Bone Structure

BLASTs Build; CLASTs Clear; CYTEs Care. Lacunae are little rooms; canaliculi connect them.

Precision check: Hydroxyapatite is inorganic mineral. Spongy bone is mineralized tissue with spaces, not soft unmineralized bone.

6.4 Bone Formation and Development

“Resting People Mature and Calcify” = Reserve, Proliferation, Maturation/hypertrophy, Calcification. “Intra in membrane; Endo replaces cartilage.”

Precision check: Cartilage does not simply turn into bone cell-for-cell. The cartilage model is replaced by newly formed osseous tissue.

6.5 Fractures: Bone Repair

“Hematoma Softens; Bone Remodels” = Hematoma, Soft callus, Bony callus, Remodeling.

Precision check: A soft callus does not have the mechanical strength of mature remodeled bone. Open reduction refers to a surgical approach, not necessarily a fracture through skin.

6.6 Exercise, Nutrition, Hormones, and Bone Tissue

Vitamin C builds Collagen; Vitamin D supports Digestive calcium uptake. “Load lays bone” recalls the response to appropriate mechanical demand.

Precision check: The study goal is understanding nutrient and hormone functions, not memorizing supplement doses. Bone quantity and mineralization quality are different variables.

6.7 Calcium Homeostasis: Interactions of the Skeletal System and Other Organ Systems

PTH = Puts calcium Higher. CalciTONin can Tone down blood calcium. Always attach the cue to a target organ and mechanism.

Precision check: PTH’s stimulation of osteoclast-mediated resorption is indirect. Blood calcium concentration is not a direct measure of total bone strength.
