25.1 Physical Characteristics of Urine

Describe before interpreting. Explain why concentration varies.

25.2 Gross Anatomy of Urine Transport

Trace urine outward. Separate storage from elimination control.

25.3 Gross Anatomy of the Kidney

Draw a kidney section. Trace papilla to ureter.

25.4 Microscopic Anatomy of the Kidney

PCT to loop to DCT to collecting duct. Match segments with vessels.

25.5 Physiology of Urine Formation

Excreted = filtered - reabsorbed + secreted. Work an example with consistent units.

25.6 Tubular Reabsorption

Descending water; ascending salt as an overview. Explain concentration mechanisms with segment details.

25.7 Regulation of Renal Blood Flow

Pressure and delivery feedback. Predict changing afferent resistance.

25.8 Endocrine Regulation of Kidney Function

ADH adds water permeability; aldosterone favors sodium retention. Compare direct targets.

25.9 Regulation of Fluid Volume and Composition

Volume and concentration are different variables. Explain how they can change independently.

25.10 The Urinary System and Homeostasis

Connect each renal function to another system. Predict consequences of losing multiple functions.
