Chapter 11 memory cues

11.1 Interactions of Skeletal Muscles, Their Fascicle Arrangement, and Their Lever Systems

Middle component: 1 fulcrum, 2 load, 3 effort.

Precision check: Origin is not absolutely immovable in every activity.

11.2 Naming Skeletal Muscles

Rectus straight; oblique angled; transversus across; biceps two heads; triceps three.

Precision check: A muscle name does not state every action or attachment.

11.3 Axial Muscles of the Head, Neck, and Back

LR6 SO4, remaining extraocular movement muscles 3. I Love Spine = Iliocostalis, Longissimus, Spinalis, lateral to medial.

Precision check: Eye-muscle action depends on eye position; do not treat a mnemonic as a complete movement description.

11.4 Axial Muscles of the Abdominal Wall, and Thorax

External oblique fibers resemble hands-in-pockets direction.

Precision check: Quiet expiration usually relies largely on passive recoil, not forceful contraction of all expiratory muscles.

11.5 Muscles of the Pectoral Girdle and Upper Limbs

SITS = Supraspinatus, Infraspinatus, Teres minor, Subscapularis. PAD/DAB = Palmar interossei ADduct, Dorsal interossei ABduct.

Precision check: Teres major is not a rotator-cuff muscle.

11.6 Appendicular Muscles of the Pelvic Girdle and Lower Limbs

Quadriceps = Rectus femoris and Vastus lateralis, medialis, intermedius. Hamstrings = Biceps femoris, Semitendinosus, Semimembranosus.

Precision check: The short head of biceps femoris does not cross the hip.
