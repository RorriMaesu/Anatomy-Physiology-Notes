# Chapter 10 memory cues

## 10.1 Overview of Muscle Tissues

Excite, contract, extend, recoil.

**Precision check:** Contraction does not always mean visible shortening.

## 10.2 Skeletal Muscle

Epi entire muscle; peri packet or fascicle; endo each fiber.

**Precision check:** A muscle fiber is a cell; a myofibril is a structure within it.

## 10.3 Muscle Fiber Contraction and Relaxation

Calcium uncovers; ATP detaches and resets.

**Precision check:** Actin and myosin slide; they do not become shorter filaments.

## 10.4 Nervous System Control of Muscle Tension

Recruitment recruits units; summation stacks responses over time.

**Precision check:** Greater whole-muscle force does not require a taller individual action potential.

## 10.5 Types of Muscle Fibers

Slow sustains; fast glycolytic fires forcefully and fatigues faster.

**Precision check:** A muscle is not necessarily made of only one fiber type.

## 10.6 Exercise and Muscle Performance

Hypertrophy bigger cells; hyperplasia more cells.

**Precision check:** Delayed soreness is not simply retained lactic acid.

## 10.7 Cardiac Muscle Tissue

Desmosomes hold; gap junctions communicate.

**Precision check:** Each heartbeat does not require a somatic motor-neuron command.

## 10.8 Smooth Muscle

Smooth uses CaM: calcium-calmodulin, then kinase.

**Precision check:** Do not put troponin into the smooth-muscle regulatory pathway.

## 10.9 Development and Regeneration of Muscle Tissue

Myoblasts merge; satellite cells support repair.

**Precision check:** The three muscle types do not have equal repair capacity.
