27.1 Anatomy and Physiology of the Testicular Reproductive System

Sertoli supports; Leydig makes testosterone. Trace sperm and distinguish gland secretions.

27.2 Anatomy and Physiology of the Ovarian Reproductive System

Follicular, ovulation, luteal. Align events, uterine changes, and hormone patterns.

27.3 Development of the Male and Female Reproductive Systems

Draw developmental pathways. Distinguish chromosomes, gonads, hormones, anatomy, and gender.
