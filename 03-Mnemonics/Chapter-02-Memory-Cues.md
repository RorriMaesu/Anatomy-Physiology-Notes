# Chapter 2 memory cues

## 2.1 Elements and Atoms: The Building Blocks of Matter

P is Positive; N is Neutral. “Mass = Massive nuclear particles” recalls protons plus neutrons. CHON names the major body elements.

**Precision check:** Do not confuse mass number of one isotope with an element’s average atomic mass. Changing protons changes the element.

## 2.2 Chemical Bonds

Covalent = Co-own electrons. ANion is Negative. Hydrogen bonds Hold neighbors together.

**Precision check:** The O–H bonds within one water molecule are covalent. A hydrogen bond between water molecules is a different interaction.

## 2.3 Chemical Reactions

Synthesis Stacks; Decomposition Divides; Exchange Exchanges. Enzymes Ease the energy barrier.

**Precision check:** An enzyme changes the rate, not the reaction’s overall energy difference or its equilibrium position.

## 2.4 Inorganic Compounds Essential to Human Functioning

Hydrolysis = Hydro helps split. “Lower pH, more H” recalls direction. One pH step is tenfold.

**Precision check:** A buffer limits change; it does not make every solution neutral or prevent all possible pH changes.

## 2.5 Organic Compounds Essential to Human Functioning

Carbs fuel, Lipids store and line, Proteins perform, Nucleic acids encode. “Sequence → Shape → Service” connects protein levels to function.

**Precision check:** Lipids are not all polymers of a single repeating monomer. A steroid has fused rings, whereas a triglyceride has glycerol plus three fatty acids.
