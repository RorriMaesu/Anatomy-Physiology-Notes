Chapter 21 memory cues

Mnemonics7 sectionsPrint friendly

21.1 Anatomy of the Lymphatic and Immune Systems

Nodes filter lymph; spleen filters blood. Trace fluid back to circulation.

21.2 Barrier Defenses and the Innate Immune Response

Barrier first, rapid response next. Distinguish innate recognition from adaptive specificity.

21.3 The Adaptive Immune Response: T lymphocytes and Their Functional Types

CD4 helps; CD8 kills infected targets. Explain recognition requirements.

21.4 The Adaptive Immune Response: B-lymphocytes and Antibodies

Plasma cells produce antibodies. Compare primary and secondary responses.

21.5 The Immune Response against Pathogens

Location shapes defense. Explain why antibodies alone do not solve every infection.

21.6 Diseases Associated with Depressed or Overactive Immune Responses

Too little, misdirected, excessive. Classify a mechanism, not just a disease name.

21.7 Transplantation and Cancer Immunology

Recognition determines response. Explain transplantation's immune consequences.
