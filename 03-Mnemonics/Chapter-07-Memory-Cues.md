# Chapter 7 memory cues

## 7.1 Divisions of the Skeletal System

Axial = Axis; Appendicular = Appendages and attachments. Remember 80 + 126 = 206 as the standard adult count.

**Precision check:** A girdle belongs to the appendicular skeleton even though it lies close to the trunk. Bone counts vary with age and individual anatomy.

## 7.2 The Skull

“Old People From Texas Eat Spiders” = Occipital, Parietal, Frontal, Temporal, Ethmoid, Sphenoid. These are SIX TYPES; paired P and T give EIGHT bones.

**Precision check:** Superior and middle nasal conchae are parts of the ethmoid; the inferior nasal conchae are separate bones.

## 7.3 The Vertebral Column

Breakfast at 7, lunch at 12, dinner at 5 = C7, T12, L5. Atlas carries the head; Axis supplies the rotation pivot.

**Precision check:** The vertebral foramen carries the spinal cord region; intervertebral foramina provide routes for spinal nerves. Do not interchange them.

## 7.4 The Thoracic Cage

7 true, 3 indirect, 2 floating = 12 pairs. “Manubrium Begins, Xiphoid Ends” helps orient the sternum.

**Precision check:** Floating ribs are a subset of false ribs, not an additional group beyond the twelve pairs.

## 7.5 Embryonic Development of the Axial Skeleton

Vault forms in a membrane; Base begins with cartilage. “Fontanelles Flex for birth and growth.”

**Precision check:** Do not memorize a single closure age as an exact deadline for every person. Different sutures and fontanelles have different developmental schedules.
