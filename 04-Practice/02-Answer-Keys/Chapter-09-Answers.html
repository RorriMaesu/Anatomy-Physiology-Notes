Chapter 9 answer key

9.1 Classification of Joints

  • A. Synovial and diarthrosis; its ball-and-socket form permits multiaxial movement.
  • B. Amphiarthrosis.

9.2 Fibrous Joints

  • A. The periodontal ligament at a gomphosis.
  • B. An interosseous membrane forming a syndesmosis.

9.3 Cartilaginous Joints

  • A. Hyaline cartilage.
  • B. A cartilaginous symphysis.

9.4 Synovial Joints

  • A. Saddle.
  • B. Usually hyaline surface covering versus a fibrocartilaginous disc.

9.5 Types of Body Movements

  • A. Opposition.
  • B. Circumduction moves the distal end through a cone; rotation turns a bone about its long axis.

9.6 Anatomy of Selected Synovial Joints

  • A. PCL.
  • B. Deeper socket and strong capsule, ligaments, and muscular support suited to weight bearing.

9.7 Development of Joints

  • A. Articular hyaline cartilage.
  • B. Fibrous connective tissue.
