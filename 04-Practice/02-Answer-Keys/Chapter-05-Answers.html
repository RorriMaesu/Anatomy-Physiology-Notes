5.1 Layers of the Skin

  • A. The stratum basale.
  • B. The epidermis has no blood vessels; deeper dermal injury can bleed.

5.2 Accessory Structures of the Skin

  • A. Eccrine sweat glands.
  • B. The shaft consists of dead keratinized cells; the growing matrix is deeper in the follicle.

5.3 Functions of the Integumentary System

  • A. It carries more heat toward the surface, supporting heat transfer to the environment.
  • B. No. It initiates the pathway; liver and kidney processing are also involved.

5.4 Diseases, Disorders, and Injuries of the Integumentary System

  • A. Damage to the barrier increases fluid loss and disrupts normal protection.
  • B. Melanoma.
