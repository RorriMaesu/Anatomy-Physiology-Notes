3.1 The Cell Membrane

  • A. No. Facilitated diffusion follows the gradient; movement against it requires another mechanism.
  • B. Water enters because the external concentration of effectively nonpenetrating solute is lower.

3.2 The Cytoplasm and Cellular Organelles

  • A. The Golgi apparatus.
  • B. Microvilli increase membrane surface area for absorption.

3.3 The Nucleus and DNA Replication

  • A. One parental strand in each new DNA double helix.
  • B. TGCA, with the two strands oriented antiparallel.

3.4 Protein Synthesis

  • A. It matches its anticodon to an mRNA codon and delivers the corresponding amino acid.
  • B. Transcription occurs in the nucleus; translation occurs on ribosomes in the cytoplasm or on rough ER.

3.5 Cell Growth and Division

  • A. S phase of interphase.
  • B. Sister chromatids, which move toward opposite poles.

3.6 Cellular Differentiation

  • A. They express different combinations of genes and consequently produce different proteins and structures.
  • B. Division that preserves a stem-cell population by producing at least one stem-cell descendant.
