Chapter 4 answer key

4.1 Types of Tissues

  • A. The synovial membrane, a connective tissue membrane.
  • B. Mucous membranes line passages opening to the exterior; serous membranes line closed ventral cavities and cover their organs.

4.2 Epithelial Tissue

  • A. Its thin barrier supports rapid diffusion.
  • B. Every cell contacts the basement membrane, although not all reach the free surface.

4.3 Connective Tissue Supports and Protects

  • A. Parallel collagen bundles resist strong tension along a main axis.
  • B. Fibrocartilage, particularly in the annulus fibrosus.

4.4 Muscle Tissue and Motion

  • A. Cardiac muscle.
  • B. Smooth cells are spindle-shaped, nonstriated, and have one central nucleus; skeletal fibers are cylindrical, striated, and multinucleated.

4.5 Nervous Tissue Mediates Perception and Response

  • A. The axon and its terminals.
  • B. Glia support, regulate, protect, and insulate parts of the signaling system.

4.6 Tissue Injury and Aging

  • A. Fibrosis can fill the defect with scar that lacks the original tissue’s specialized function.
  • B. Yes. Mechanical, thermal, or other sterile damage can trigger it.
