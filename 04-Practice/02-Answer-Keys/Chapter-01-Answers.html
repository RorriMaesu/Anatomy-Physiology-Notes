1.1 Overview of Anatomy and Physiology

  • A. Physiology, because the question asks how an organ functions.

1.2 Structural Organization of the Human Body

  • A. It contains multiple tissue types working together, including epithelium, connective tissue, smooth muscle, and nervous tissue.
  • B. The organ system.

1.3 Functions of Human Life

  • A. Anabolic: smaller glucose units are assembled into a larger storage molecule.
  • B. Cilia moving mucus, the heart pumping blood, or a cell moving vesicles internally.

1.4 Requirements for Human Life

  • A. It can alter protein structure and interfere with enzyme activity and other cell functions.
