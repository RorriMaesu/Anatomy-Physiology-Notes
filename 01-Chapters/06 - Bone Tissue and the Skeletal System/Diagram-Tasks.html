Chapter 6 diagram tasks

Chapter notesNotes & drawing tasks7 sectionsPrint friendly

These are drawing assignments to create while studying. Ready-made labeled and blank schematics are in 02-Diagrams. Use textbook figures for anatomical detail.

6.1 The Functions of the Skeletal System

Make a function–anatomical example–system partner table. Connect vertebrae to protection, femur to support and movement, and marrow to blood-cell formation.

OpenStax section 6.1

6.2 Bone Classification

Create a five-shape table with definition, two examples where available, and functional relationship. Sort a mixed list of bones without looking at the table.

OpenStax section 6.2

6.3 Bone Structure

Draw a long bone and enlarged osteon, then compare compact and spongy tissue. Make a bone-marking glossary with one real example per marking.

OpenStax section 6.3

6.4 Bone Formation and Development

Draw side-by-side ossification timelines and an enlarged growth plate oriented from epiphysis to diaphysis. Show periosteal deposition and endosteal resorption during widening.

OpenStax section 6.4

6.5 Fractures: Bone Repair

Draw four fracture-repair panels with tissue type and participating cells. Sketch each fracture pattern from the textbook and add one distinguishing sentence.

OpenStax section 6.5

6.6 Exercise, Nutrition, Hormones, and Bone Tissue

Make a factor–target–effect table for mechanical load, nutrient roles, growth hormones, and calcium-regulating hormones. Compare osteoporosis with osteomalacia or rickets at the mechanism level.

OpenStax section 6.6

6.7 Calcium Homeostasis: Interactions of the Skeletal System and Other Organ Systems

Draw a low-calcium negative-feedback loop with three target branches: bone, kidney, intestine via calcitriol. Use arrows to show calcium entering or leaving blood.

OpenStax section 6.7
