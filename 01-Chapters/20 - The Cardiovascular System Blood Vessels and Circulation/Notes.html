20.1 Structure and Function of Blood Vessels

OpenStax section 20.1

Notes and diagram to make: Vessel tunics, arteries, arterioles, capillaries, venules, and veins.

Memory cue and retrieval target: Arteries away; veins toward the heart. Connect wall structure to pressure handling.
