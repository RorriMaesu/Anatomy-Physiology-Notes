19.1 Heart Anatomy

OpenStax section 19.1

Notes and diagram to make: Chambers, valves, heart layers, great vessels, and coronary circulation.

Memory cue and retrieval target: Right to lungs; left to body. Trace blood through chambers and valves.

19.2 Cardiac Muscle and Electrical Activity

OpenStax section 19.2

Notes and diagram to make: Autorhythmic and contractile cells, conduction system, and ECG relationships.

Memory cue and retrieval target: SA to AV to bundle to branches to Purkinje. Distinguish electrical and mechanical events.

19.3 Cardiac Cycle

OpenStax section 19.3

Notes and diagram to make: Filling, contraction, ejection, relaxation, pressure changes, and valve states.

Memory cue and retrieval target: Pressure opens and closes valves. Predict valve state from pressure differences.

19.4 Cardiac Physiology

OpenStax section 19.4

Notes and diagram to make: Heart rate, stroke volume, cardiac output, preload, contractility, and afterload.

Memory cue and retrieval target: CO = HR x SV. Explain each variable's distinct effect.

OpenStax section 19.5

Notes and diagram to make: Heart tube, looping, septation, and major developmental transitions.

Memory cue and retrieval target: Draw sequential stages. Explain how a partitioning defect changes flow.
