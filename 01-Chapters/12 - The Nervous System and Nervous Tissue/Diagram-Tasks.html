Chapter 12 diagram tasks

Chapter notesNotes & drawing tasks5 sectionsPrint friendly

These are drawing assignments to create while studying. Ready-made labeled and blank schematics are in 02-Diagrams. Use textbook figures for anatomical detail.

12.1 Basic Structure and Function of the Nervous System

Make separate anatomical and functional classification maps. Build a CNS/PNS terminology table.

OpenStax section 12.1

12.2 Nervous Tissue

Draw neurons and myelin; build a six-glial-cell table with location and function.

OpenStax section 12.2

12.3 The Function of Nervous Tissue

Draw the textbook warm-water example with structures and signal types. Add a reflex comparison.

OpenStax section 12.3

12.4 The Action Potential

Draw voltage versus time, with resting potential, threshold, rise, fall, and undershoot. Add channel states and ion directions. Textbook voltages are representative, not universal.

OpenStax section 12.4

12.5 Communication Between Neurons

Draw a chemical synapse and a summation comparison. Build transmitter-family and graded/action-potential tables.

OpenStax section 12.5
