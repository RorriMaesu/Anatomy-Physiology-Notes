Chapter 12 diagram tasks

These are drawing assignments to create while studying. Ready-made labeled and blank schematics are in 02-Diagrams. Use textbook figures for anatomical detail.

Make separate anatomical and functional classification maps. Build a CNS/PNS terminology table.

12.4 The Action Potential

Draw voltage versus time, with resting potential, threshold, rise, fall, and undershoot. Add channel states and ion directions. Textbook voltages are representative, not universal.
