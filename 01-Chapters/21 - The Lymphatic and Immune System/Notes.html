21.1 Anatomy of the Lymphatic and Immune Systems

OpenStax section 21.1

Notes and diagram to make: Lymph vessels, nodes, ducts, spleen, thymus, and lymphoid tissues.

Memory cue and retrieval target: Nodes filter lymph; spleen filters blood. Trace fluid back to circulation.
