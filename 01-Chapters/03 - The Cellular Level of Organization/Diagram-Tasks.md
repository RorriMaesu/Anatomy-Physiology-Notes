# Chapter 3 diagram tasks

These are drawing assignments to create while studying. Ready-made labeled and blank schematics are in 02-Diagrams. Use textbook figures for anatomical detail.

## 3.1 The Cell Membrane

Draw the bilayer and a transport comparison chart with cargo, direction, protein requirement, and energy source. Sketch cells in three tonicities and label the solution relative to the cell.

[OpenStax section 3.1](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/3-1-the-cell-membrane)

## 3.2 The Cytoplasm and Cellular Organelles

Make an organelle–structure–function table. Trace a secreted protein from ribosome to rough ER, transport vesicle, Golgi, secretory vesicle, and plasma membrane. Compare cilia with microvilli.

[OpenStax section 3.2](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/3-2-the-cytoplasm-and-cellular-organelles)

## 3.3 The Nucleus and DNA Replication

Draw a nucleus, a DNA ladder, and a replication fork. Mark old versus new strands, direction, leading/lagging strands, polymerase, and ligase. Practice complementary sequences.

[OpenStax section 3.3](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/3-3-the-nucleus-and-dna-replication)

## 3.4 Protein Synthesis

Draw nucleus-to-cytoplasm information flow. Practice one short example: DNA template 3′-TAC-5′ → mRNA 5′-AUG-3′ → methionine. Label transcription and translation at different locations.

[OpenStax section 3.4](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/3-4-protein-synthesis)

## 3.5 Cell Growth and Division

Draw a circular cell-cycle map and four mitotic panels. Track one homologous pair using two colors, then show duplication and distribution. Add checkpoints and distinguish mitosis from cytokinesis.

[OpenStax section 3.5](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/3-5-cell-growth-and-division)

## 3.6 Cellular Differentiation

Make a potency hierarchy with definitions and examples from the section. Compare a neuron and muscle fiber under genes present, genes expressed, structure, and function.

[OpenStax section 3.6](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/3-6-cellular-differentiation)
