Chapter 5 diagram tasks

These are drawing assignments to create while studying. Ready-made labeled and blank schematics are in 02-Diagrams. Use textbook figures for anatomical detail.

5.1 Layers of the Skin

Draw a skin section with every stratum, both dermal layers, hypodermis, vessels, and major cell types. Compare thick and thin skin. Connect surface cell loss with basal replacement.

OpenStax section 5.1

5.2 Accessory Structures of the Skin

Make a hair-and-nail labeling page plus a gland comparison table with secretion, outlet, location, and function. Draw an eccrine duct to the surface and sebaceous duct to a follicle.

OpenStax section 5.2

5.3 Functions of the Integumentary System

Draw warm- and cold-response feedback loops. Make a structure–function table linking keratin, melanin, blood vessels, glands, receptors, and adipose tissue to their jobs.

OpenStax section 5.3

5.4 Diseases, Disorders, and Injuries of the Integumentary System

Create disorder–affected structure–mechanism–functional consequence notes. Draw three burn depths. Learn the adult rule-of-nines diagram if assigned, including its limits for children.
