Chapter 9 diagram tasks

These are drawing assignments to create while studying. Ready-made labeled and blank schematics are in 02-Diagrams. Use textbook figures for anatomical detail.

9.1 Classification of Joints

Give skull sutures, pubic symphysis, shoulder, elbow, and a syndesmosis both structural and functional labels.

OpenStax section 9.1

9.2 Fibrous Joints

Draw a suture, gomphosis, and syndesmosis; label the connecting tissue and explain stability versus mobility.

OpenStax section 9.2

9.3 Cartilaginous Joints

Build a cartilage-type, example, and movement table. Draw growth plate, pubic symphysis, and intervertebral disc.

OpenStax section 9.3

9.4 Synovial Joints

Draw a generic joint. Build a six-shape matrix with example, shape, axes, and permitted movement.

OpenStax section 9.4

9.5 Types of Body Movements

Demonstrate each movement safely and draw its direction on an anatomical outline. Include special reference axes for fingers and toes.

OpenStax section 9.5

9.6 Anatomy of Selected Synovial Joints

Make one page per major joint: bones, subtype, movements, stabilizers, and injury mechanism. Draw knee ligaments with tibia and femur identified.

OpenStax section 9.6

9.7 Development of Joints

Draw mesenchyme, cartilage models, interzone, and mature joint. Compare tissue retained at sutures and synovial surfaces.

OpenStax section 9.7
