26.1 Body Fluids and Fluid Compartments

OpenStax section 26.1

Notes and diagram to make: Intracellular/extracellular fluid, plasma/interstitial fluid, and solute distribution.

OpenStax section 26.2

Notes and diagram to make: Water intake/loss, thirst, ADH, and osmotic shifts.

Memory cue and retrieval target: Water follows effective osmotic gradients. Predict cell-volume change.

26.3 Electrolyte Balance

OpenStax section 26.3

Notes and diagram to make: Sodium, potassium, calcium, phosphate, chloride, magnesium, and regulation.

Memory cue and retrieval target: Na+ mostly outside; K+ mostly inside. Explain concentration effects on excitability.

26.4 Acid-Base Balance

OpenStax section 26.4

Notes and diagram to make: Chemical buffers, lungs, kidneys, and bicarbonate relationships.

Memory cue and retrieval target: Buffers first; lungs fast; kidneys slower and sustained. Explain each mechanism.

26.5 Disorders of Acid-Base Balance

OpenStax section 26.5

Notes and diagram to make: Respiratory/metabolic disturbances, compensation, and pH/CO2/HCO3 patterns.

Memory cue and retrieval target: ROME: Respiratory Opposite, Metabolic Equal, comparing pH direction with CO2 or bicarbonate. Explain its limits.
