Chapter 13 diagram tasks

Chapter notesNotes & drawing tasks4 sectionsPrint friendly

These are drawing assignments to create while studying. Ready-made labeled and blank schematics are in 02-Diagrams. Use textbook figures for anatomical detail.

13.1 The Embryologic Perspective

Neural tube, neural crest, primary and secondary brain vesicles, adult derivatives, and ventricular relationships. Draw a vesicle-to-adult map.

OpenStax section 13.1

13.2 The Central Nervous System

Cerebral lobes, cortex, basal nuclei, diencephalon, brainstem, cerebellum, and spinal cord; map regional functions.

OpenStax section 13.2

13.3 Circulation and the Central Nervous System

Meninges, arterial supply, venous drainage, barriers, and CSF production/circulation. Draw blood and CSF routes separately.

OpenStax section 13.3

13.4 The Peripheral Nervous System

Cranial and spinal nerves, roots, rami, ganglia, plexuses, and nerve coverings. Draw root-to-nerve-to-ramus relationships.

OpenStax section 13.4
