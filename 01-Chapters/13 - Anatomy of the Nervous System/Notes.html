Chapter 13 Anatomy of the Nervous System

Planning outline. Match these sections to your actual reading assignments.

13.1 The Embryologic Perspective

OpenStax section 13.1

Notes and diagram to make: Neural tube, neural crest, primary and secondary brain vesicles, adult derivatives, and ventricular relationships. Draw a vesicle-to-adult map.

Memory cue and retrieval target: Forebrain divides into telencephalon and diencephalon. Retrieve each vesicle's adult derivative.

13.2 The Central Nervous System

OpenStax section 13.2

Notes and diagram to make: Cerebral lobes, cortex, basal nuclei, diencephalon, brainstem, cerebellum, and spinal cord; map regional functions.

Memory cue and retrieval target: Midbrain, pons, medulla form the brainstem. Identify a region and predict a consequence of damage.

13.3 Circulation and the Central Nervous System

OpenStax section 13.3

Notes and diagram to make: Meninges, arterial supply, venous drainage, barriers, and CSF production/circulation. Draw blood and CSF routes separately.

Memory cue and retrieval target: DAP = Dura, Arachnoid, Pia, superficial to deep. Trace CSF and distinguish it from blood flow.

13.4 The Peripheral Nervous System

OpenStax section 13.4

Notes and diagram to make: Cranial and spinal nerves, roots, rami, ganglia, plexuses, and nerve coverings. Draw root-to-nerve-to-ramus relationships.

Memory cue and retrieval target: Dorsal sensory, ventral motor roots. Explain why a spinal nerve contains both.
