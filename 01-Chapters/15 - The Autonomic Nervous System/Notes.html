15.1 Divisions of the Autonomic Nervous System

OpenStax section 15.1

Notes and diagram to make: Sympathetic and parasympathetic origins, ganglia, fiber lengths, transmitters, receptors, and exceptions. Draw both two-neuron chains.

Memory cue and retrieval target: Thoracolumbar sympathetic; craniosacral parasympathetic. Compare chain anatomy.

15.2 Autonomic Reflexes and Homeostasis

OpenStax section 15.2

Notes and diagram to make: Visceral reflex arcs, autonomic tone, dual innervation, and organ responses.

Memory cue and retrieval target: Same organ, different receptor effects. Predict heart, pupil, airway, and digestive responses.

15.3 Central Control

OpenStax section 15.3

Notes and diagram to make: Hypothalamic, brainstem, spinal, and limbic influences on autonomic activity.

Memory cue and retrieval target: Hypothalamus coordinates homeostasis. Connect emotion to an autonomic response.

15.4 Drugs that Affect the Autonomic System

OpenStax section 15.4

Notes and diagram to make: Agonists, antagonists, cholinergic and adrenergic receptors, and textbook drug mechanisms.

Memory cue and retrieval target: Agonist activates; antagonist blocks. Predict effects from receptor action.
