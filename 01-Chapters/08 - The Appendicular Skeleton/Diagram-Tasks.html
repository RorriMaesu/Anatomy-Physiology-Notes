Chapter 8 diagram tasks

These are drawing assignments to create while studying. Ready-made labeled and blank schematics are in 02-Diagrams. Use textbook figures for anatomical detail.

8.1 The Pectoral Girdle

Draw anterior and posterior scapular views and both clavicular ends. Practice siding a scapula: glenoid lateral, spine posterior, inferior angle down. List the articulating partners.

OpenStax section 8.1

8.2 Bones of the Upper Limb

Draw anterior/posterior humerus, both forearm bones, and a palmar hand. Practice siding bones and naming their joint partners. Mark carpals in rows from thumb to little finger.

OpenStax section 8.2

8.3 The Pelvic Girdle and Pelvis

Draw one hip bone in medial and lateral views and the complete pelvis. Color the three developmental components. Compare load-bearing features with the pectoral girdle.

OpenStax section 8.3

8.4 Bones of the Lower Limb

Draw femur, tibia/fibula, and superior/inferior foot views. Number digits from great toe I. Trace the route of body weight through tibia, talus, and foot.

OpenStax section 8.4

8.5 Development of the Appendicular Skeleton

Draw a limb-bud timeline: bud, paddle, separated digits, cartilage model, ossification, growth plate, fusion. Compare upper-limb lateral rotation with lower-limb medial rotation.
