Chapter 10 diagram tasks

Chapter notesNotes & drawing tasks9 sectionsPrint friendly

These are drawing assignments to create while studying. Ready-made labeled and blank schematics are in 02-Diagrams. Use textbook figures for anatomical detail.

10.1 Overview of Muscle Tissues

Rebuild the three-muscle comparison table, then add functional properties and examples.

OpenStax section 10.1

10.2 Skeletal Muscle

Draw nested organization, a labeled sarcomere, and an NMJ. Trace force through connective tissue to a tendon.

OpenStax section 10.2

10.3 Muscle Fiber Contraction and Relaxation

Draw excitation-contraction coupling, the cross-bridge cycle, and a relaxed/shortened sarcomere. Make an ATP-source comparison.

OpenStax section 10.3

10.4 Nervous System Control of Muscle Tension

Draw twitch and summation traces. Compare more active motor units with more frequent stimulation.

OpenStax section 10.4

10.5 Types of Muscle Fibers

Make a three-row fiber matrix and connect each pattern to an activity.

OpenStax section 10.5

10.6 Exercise and Muscle Performance

Make an endurance/resistance comparison at the cellular level. Explain disuse and aging effects without treating all changes as inevitable.

OpenStax section 10.6

10.7 Cardiac Muscle Tissue

Draw intercalated-disc connections and compare skeletal with cardiac activation.

OpenStax section 10.7

10.8 Smooth Muscle

Compare skeletal and smooth regulation in parallel pathways. Label calmodulin and kinase rather than troponin.

OpenStax section 10.8

10.9 Development and Regeneration of Muscle Tissue

Make a development and repair comparison for skeletal, cardiac, and smooth tissue.
