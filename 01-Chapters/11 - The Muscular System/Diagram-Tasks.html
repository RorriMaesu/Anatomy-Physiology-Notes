Chapter 11 diagram tasks

Chapter notesNotes & drawing tasks6 sectionsPrint friendly

These are drawing assignments to create while studying. Ready-made labeled and blank schematics are in 02-Diagrams. Use textbook figures for anatomical detail.

11.1 Interactions of Skeletal Muscles, Their Fascicle Arrangement, and Their Lever Systems

Analyze one movement by muscle roles. Draw first-, second-, and third-class levers with real examples.

OpenStax section 11.1

11.2 Naming Skeletal Muscles

Make a word-part table and explain each example aloud. Add the anatomical information the name does not provide.

OpenStax section 11.2

11.3 Axial Muscles of the Head, Neck, and Back

Draw front/side head and neck, eye movements, and posterior trunk. Build muscle records from the section figures.

OpenStax section 11.3

11.4 Axial Muscles of the Abdominal Wall, and Thorax

Draw abdominal layers, diaphragm before/after contraction, and pelvic-floor support. Add assigned origin/insertion/action records.

OpenStax section 11.4

11.5 Muscles of the Pectoral Girdle and Upper Limbs

Draw anterior/posterior upper limb and hand. Build movement-first groups, then origins, insertions, and assigned nerves.

OpenStax section 11.5

11.6 Appendicular Muscles of the Pelvic Girdle and Lower Limbs

Draw anterior/posterior lower limb and a compartment-action map. Create origin/insertion/action records for each assigned muscle.

OpenStax section 11.6
