Chapter 2 diagram tasks

Chapter notesNotes & drawing tasks5 sectionsPrint friendly

These are drawing assignments to create while studying. Ready-made labeled and blank schematics are in 02-Diagrams. Use textbook figures for anatomical detail.

2.1 Elements and Atoms: The Building Blocks of Matter

Draw a simple atom and label charges and locations. Calculate proton, neutron, and electron counts for carbon-12 and a sodium ion. Compare element, compound, isotope, and ion.

OpenStax section 2.1

2.2 Chemical Bonds

Sketch electron transfer for sodium/chloride, shared electrons for a covalent bond, and two water molecules connected by a hydrogen bond. Mark full ionic charges separately from partial charges.

OpenStax section 2.2

2.3 Chemical Reactions

Write one symbolic example each of synthesis, decomposition, exchange, and reversibility. Draw an energy diagram with and without an enzyme and label activation energy.

OpenStax section 2.3

2.4 Inorganic Compounds Essential to Human Functioning

Build a water-functions table and a pH number line. Work pH comparisons of 7 versus 6 and 7 versus 5. Draw a buffer accepting excess H+.

OpenStax section 2.4

2.5 Organic Compounds Essential to Human Functioning

Create a four-family table with building blocks, bonds or structure, major functions, and examples. Sketch a phospholipid and nucleotide. Add DNA/RNA sugar and base comparisons.
